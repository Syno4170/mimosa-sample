Our story

Charm of the French Riviera, spirit of the Red Sea.

A modern Mediterranean steakhouse celebrating coastal flavours, fresh ingredients and refined simplicity.

Born from a Parisian concept, Mimosa brings sun-washed Mediterranean dining to Cascade at Jeddah Walk — hand-painted plates, sage banquettes, a garden terrace lit by candlelight, and a grill that knows exactly what it's doing. Lunch lingers into dinner; dinner lingers into the night.
